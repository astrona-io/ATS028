# Summary

An application must be accepted, placed and prepared before its process can run. Scheduling evidence concerns requests, eligibility and capacity, while preparation evidence concerns artifacts, mounts and startup inputs.

Changing routing does not repair a missing image, and lowering requests without understanding real needs can move failure into runtime. Diagnose the earliest failed condition and preserve the intended application requirements.

Pending, waiting, running and terminated states need interpretation rather than one universal remedy. Placement, image access, volume preparation and process startup each provide different evidence and require a correction at the failed boundary.
